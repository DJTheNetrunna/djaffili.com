Which SSD fits your PC?

  1. Find the exact model of your laptop or motherboard and look up its storage specifications.
  2. Check the interface. A 2.5-inch SATA bay uses a different connection than an M.2 slot. An M.2 slot can support NVMe, SATA, or both depending on the system.
  3. Check physical fit. Match the M.2 length or confirm space for a 2.5-inch drive. For an enclosure, match both the length and protocol of the drive you already have.
  4. Back up your files and decide whether you’ll clone the existing drive or install your operating system from scratch.
